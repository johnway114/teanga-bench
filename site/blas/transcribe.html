Audio and quota privacy

Blas does not keep audio or transcripts or use them for training. Cloudflare Turnstile receives your IP address and challenge token for verification.

To enforce the daily limit, the isolated service stores a keyed hash of your IP, the UTC date and seconds used, plus a daily total. It does not store your raw IP. Old quota entries are removed when the service next accepts a request on a later UTC day, so they can remain longer if the service is idle.
