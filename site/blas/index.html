How to read this

  • Score is the share of items answered correctly, averaged over repeat asks. Translation tests give partial credit. Every test counts equally.
  • The thin line on each bar is the 95% range. When two models' ranges overlap, the gap between them may be chance.
  • Sources, not reviewers. Items and answers come from the named sources and were not reviewed by people. Tutor explanations are marked by judge models and shown separately.
