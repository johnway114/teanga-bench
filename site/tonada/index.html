What v2 measures

Four core tests. Each item is asked once, with low reasoning. Answers are marked by fixed rules (an accepted-answer list, or chrF++ for translation), so no model grades another model's headline score. Every score comes with a 95% range. The retired v1 tests are not on this board.

  • Tutor accuracy. Find and fix the error in an English sentence, or leave a correct sentence alone; an item counts only when the error is found and fixed and nothing else is changed. 360 items.

How to read this

  • Score is the share of items answered correctly, averaged over repeat asks. Translation tests give partial credit. Every test counts equally.
  • The thin line on each bar is the 95% range. When two models' ranges overlap, the gap between them may be chance.
  • Sources, not reviewers. Items and answers come from the named sources and were not reviewed by people. Tutor explanations are marked by judge models and shown separately.
